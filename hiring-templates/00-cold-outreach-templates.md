# Cold Outreach Templates — Founding Engineer

Templates for recruiting a founding engineer with procedural generation/simulation experience. Personalize all `[bracketed placeholders]` with candidate-specific details before sending.

## Variant 1: LinkedIn InMail — Technical Angle

> **Channel:** LinkedIn InMail  
> **Angle:** Hard engineering challenge of procedural world generation  
> **Tone:** Technical, direct, peer-to-peer  
> **Target length:** < 200 words

---

Hi [Candidate Name],

I came across your work on [specific project — e.g., "your procedural terrain engine" / "the simulation work at X" / "your open-source world gen library"], and it's exactly the kind of thing we're building at [Company].

We're hiring a founding engineer to tackle a genuinely hard problem: a server that simulates Earth-like planets at scale — geological uplift, hydrological cycles, ecosystem建模, and millennia of simulated history, all running concurrently with configurable species behaviors and eventual multiplayer agent support.

It's the kind of project where performance profiling *is* the design work, and where a single architectural decision ripples through every layer of the stack.

If that kind of constraint-driven engineering challenges you, I'd love to chat.

[Book a 20-min call](https://calendly.com) or reply here.

— [Your Name], [Title] at [Company]

## Quick Personalization Checklist

Before sending any variant, verify:

- [ ] Candidate name is correct (check spelling)
- [ ] Specific project/achievement referenced is accurate (do not guess)
- [ ] Channel-appropriate tone is maintained (InMail ≠ Discord DM)
- [ ] Call-to-action link is live and personalized
- [ ] Company name and your role/title are current
- [ ] Reference to candidate's background is specific, not generic

## Sending Cadence

| Action | Timing |
|--------|--------|
| Initial outreach | Day 1 |
| Follow-up #1 | Day 5–7 |
| Follow-up #2 | Day 14–16 |
| Final follow-up | Day 28–30 |

Keep all follow-ups short (2–3 sentences max) and reference the original message without repeating it verbatim.
