# Interview Email Templates

## 1. Interview Confirmation (with Prep Instructions)

```
Subject: Interview Confirmed: [Position] at [Company] - [Date/Time]

Hi [Candidate Name],

Great news! We'd love to move forward with your interview for the [Position Title] role.

**Interview Details:**
- Date: [Day, Month Date, Year]
- Time: [Time] [AM/PM] [Timezone]
- Format: [Video Call / Phone / In-Person]
- Duration: [Duration]

**Interviewer:** [Interviewer Name], [Title]

**Join Link:** [Video call link or address]

**How to Prepare:**
- [Any technical requirements - coding platform access, etc.]
- [Dress code if in-person]
- [Materials to review - job description, company info, etc.]
- [Format details - system design, behavioral, etc.]

Please confirm you can attend by replying to this email. If you need to reschedule, let us know as soon as possible and we'll find a time that works.

Looking forward to speaking with you!

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Company Name]
```

## 2. Schedule Reminder (24hr Before)

```
Subject: Reminder: Your [Company] Interview Tomorrow at [Time]

Hi [Candidate Name],

Just a friendly reminder about your upcoming interview:

**Tomorrow, [Day, Month Date]**
- Time: [Time] [AM/PM] [Timezone]
- Role: [Position Title]
- Interviewer: [Interviewer Name]

**Join Link:** [Link]

**Need to reschedule?**
If anything has changed, please reply to let us know and we'll work it out.

See you soon!

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Company Name]
```

## 3. Rejection After Phone Screen

```
Subject: Update on Your Application - [Company]

Hi [Candidate Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with us about the [Position Title] role. We enjoyed learning about your background and experience.

After careful consideration, we've decided to move forward with other candidates whose experience more closely matches our current needs.

This was a competitive process, and we encourage you to apply for future roles that align with your skills. We'll keep your information on file for [X months].

We wish you the best in your job search and future career.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Company Name]
```

---

## 4. Rejection After Technical Round

```
Subject: Update on Your Technical Interview - [Company]

Hi [Candidate Name],

Thank you for taking the time to complete the technical portion of our interview process. We appreciate the effort you put into the [coding exercise / system design / technical discussion].

After reviewing your work, we've decided to move forward with other candidates. This was not an easy decision, as we had many strong applicants.

[Optional: Specific constructive feedback if you choose to include it]
"While we felt your [strength] was strong, we decided to move forward with candidates who had more experience with [specific area]."

We're grateful for your interest in [Company] and encourage you to apply for future positions.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Company Name]
```

---

## 5. Offer Notification

```
Subject: Exciting News: Offer for [Position] at [Company]!

Hi [Candidate Name],

I'm thrilled to share that we'd like to extend an offer for the [Position Title] role!

We were impressed by your [specific quality/experience] and believe you'll be a fantastic addition to the team.

**Offer Details:**
- Position: [Title]
- Start Date: [Date]
- Compensation: [Salary + equity details]
- [Benefits summary or attachment]

Please find the formal offer letter attached. We'd appreciate your response by [Date - typically 5 business days].

If you have any questions, I'm happy to chat. This is an exciting time, and I can't wait to welcome you aboard!

Warmly,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Company Name]
[Phone/Email]
```

---

## Usage Notes

- **Personalize each template** with candidate-specific details before sending
- **Keep rejection emails brief but respectful** - don't over-explain
- **Follow up on offers** if no response within expected timeframe
- **Track all communications** in ATS or candidate tracking system
