# Interview Calendar Invite Templates

## 1. Phone Screen (30 minutes)

```
Subject: Phone Screen Interview - [Company Name] - [Candidate Name]

Calendar Event:
- Duration: 30 minutes
- Location: [Video Call Link] or [Phone Number]
- Description:
  Thank you for your interest in the [Position Title] role at [Company Name].
  
  Agenda:
  • Introduction and background discussion (10 min)
  • Role fit assessment (15 min)  
  • Questions and next steps (5 min)
  
  Please ensure you have a stable internet connection and are in a quiet location.
  
  Video Link: [Zoom/Meet Link]
  Dial-in: [Phone Number]
```

---

## 2. Technical Interview (60-90 minutes)

```
Subject: Technical Interview - [Company Name] - [Candidate Name]

Calendar Event:
- Duration: 90 minutes (block 60-90 min)
- Location: [Video Call Link] or [Office Address + Room]
- Description:
  Technical Interview for [Position Title] role at [Company Name].
  
  Agenda:
  • Introduction (5 min)
  • Technical deep-dive / Coding Exercise (45-60 min)
  • System design discussion (20 min)
  • Your questions (10 min)
  
  For coding: You'll be given access to [CoderPad/Replit/LeetCode] - no IDE needed.
  
  Requirements:
  - Stable internet connection
  - Browser with access to [coding platform]
  - Quiet, distraction-free environment
  
  Video Link: [Zoom/Meet Link]
  Contact: [Interviewer Name] at [email/phone]
```

---

## 3. Final Round (45-60 minutes)

```
Subject: Final Interview - [Company Name] - [Candidate Name]

Calendar Event:
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Location: [Video Call Link] or [Office Address]
- Description:
  Final Round Interview for [Position Title] at [Company Name].
  
  This session will include:
  • Culture and values alignment discussion
  • Leadership / cross-functional collaboration scenarios
  • Career growth and expectations
  • Deep-dive on past experience and decision-making
  
  Interviewers:
  - [Name 1] - [Title/Role]
  - [Name 2] - [Title/Role]
  
  Video Link: [Zoom/Meet Link]
  Backup contact: [HR Contact] at [email]
```

---

## Booking Best Practices

1. **Send 48+ hours in advance** when possible
2. **Include timezone explicitly** - especially for remote candidates
3. **Provide calendar attachments** (.ics files) for easy import
4. **Add prep materials** link in description if applicable
5. **Include backup contact** in case of technical issues
6. **Set video reminders** 10 minutes before start time
